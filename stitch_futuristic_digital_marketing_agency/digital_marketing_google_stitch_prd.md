# Google Stitch Design Brief

## DIGITAL MARKETING --- Premium Animated Agency Website

**Purpose:** Use this brief in Google Stitch to generate a premium,
cohesive website UI for a digital marketing agency. Create the seven
screens/pages listed below and maintain one consistent design system
across all screens.

## 1. Brand and visual direction

-   Brand name: **DIGITAL MARKETING**
-   Tagline: **We turn your vision into digital growth**
-   Style: premium, futuristic, cinematic, polished creative agency.
-   Palette: deep black/navy background, electric purple, violet and
    neon-blue gradients, white typography, soft grey secondary text.
-   Visual language: glowing particles, subtle light beams,
    glassmorphism cards, restrained neon borders, large editorial
    typography, generous spacing, elegant hover states.
-   Avoid generic template styling, excessive rounded cards, clutter,
    and childish graphics.
-   Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## 2. Global animated background

Design a reusable background treatment for the homepage hero and
selected sections: - Recognizable floating Facebook, Instagram, Google,
SEO, AI, Google Ads, and analytics icons. - Icons drift slowly in
different directions, with varied size, opacity, speed, and gentle
rotation. - Purple/blue animated gradient, subtle glowing particles, and
radial light effects. - Keep all decorative elements behind content and
never obscure text or controls. - Animation should be subtle and
premium, not distracting. - Include a reduced-motion-friendly static
alternative.

## 3. Required seven pages

Create these seven main pages with consistent navigation and footer:

1.  Home --- `/`
2.  Services --- `/services`
3.  Our Creative Work --- `/portfolio`
4.  Our Results --- `/results`
5.  About Us --- `/about`
6.  Contact / Free Consultation --- `/contact`
7.  Insights / Blog --- `/insights`

### Shared navigation

Brand logo/text: **DIGITAL MARKETING**

Navigation links: Home · Services · Our Creative Work · Our Results ·
About Us · Contact · Insights

Primary CTA: **Let's Talk**

Use a transparent navbar over the hero and a dark glass background after
scrolling. Include a mobile hamburger menu and clear active-page state.

## 4. Page 1 --- Home

### Hero section

Show this exact copy prominently:

**DIGITAL MARKETING**

**We turn your vision into digital growth**

Buttons: - **Explore our services** → `/services` - **Let's work
together** → `/contact`

Design as a cinematic, nearly full-screen hero with bold typography,
floating marketing icons, glowing particles, purple/blue gradient
lighting, and smooth entrance animations. Keep the content readable and
the two CTA buttons obvious.

### Our Creative Work section

Place this section immediately below the hero so visitors discover it by
scrolling.

Title: **OUR CREATIVE WORK**

Supporting copy: *Creative ideas. Powerful videos. Content that makes
brands stand out.*

Create Instagram Reels-inspired vertical video cards for: - AI Generated
Videos - Promotional Videos - Product Advertisements - Business Reels -
Social Media Creatives

Each card should include a 9:16 thumbnail/poster, visible play button,
project title, category, and optional short description. Design a
video-player modal state with close control, play/pause, mute, volume,
and fullscreen controls where supported. Show loading/error/empty
states. Use placeholders when real video assets are not supplied; do not
imply placeholder videos are actual client work.

Include a **View All Work** CTA to `/portfolio`.

### Additional homepage sections

-   Six-service preview.
-   Why Choose Us.
-   Our Process: Discover → Strategize → Create → Launch → Optimize →
    Report.
-   Featured projects.
-   Final CTA: **Ready to grow your brand?**
-   Shared footer.

## 5. Page 2 --- Services (`/services`)

Title: **OUR SERVICES**

Supporting copy: *Smart digital solutions to grow your business.*

Create six premium animated cards:

1.  **Meta Ads** --- Facebook and Instagram advertising, audience
    targeting, campaign management, and optimization.
2.  **SEO & Local SEO** --- Search visibility, on-page SEO, technical
    SEO, and local search optimization.
3.  **Google Business Profile** --- Profile setup, optimization,
    business information, and local presence management.
4.  **Website Development** --- Responsive, modern websites designed to
    present a business professionally and generate inquiries.
5.  **Social Media Marketing** --- Content planning, creative design,
    publishing strategy, and account management.
6.  **AI Video Creation** --- AI-generated videos, promotional videos,
    product advertisements, and short-form creative content.

Each card needs a relevant icon, title, short description, **View
Details** link, and **Get a Quote** CTA. Use elegant scroll reveals,
subtle hover lift, and gradient-border/glow effects. Design
service-detail states/pages if supported. Do not promise guaranteed
results.

## 6. Page 3 --- Our Creative Work (`/portfolio`)

Title: **OUR CREATIVE WORK**

Create a rich portfolio gallery with filter tabs: **All · AI Videos ·
Promotional · Product Ads · Business Reels · Social Media Creatives**

Include: - Vertical 9:16 Reels cards and support for landscape videos. -
Thumbnail/poster and play button. - Project title, category, optional
description. - Click-to-play modal. - Category filtering and smooth
transitions. - Responsive grid and touch-friendly mobile layout. -
Loading, missing-video, and playback-error states.

Use real supplied media when available. Keep portfolio items easy to
replace through a structured data source. Clearly label demo content.

## 7. Page 4 --- Our Results (`/results`)

Title: **REAL RESULTS. MEANINGFUL GROWTH.**

Supporting copy: *Showcase the impact of our digital marketing campaigns
with transparent, verifiable performance data.*

Design metric cards for: - Leads Generated - Total Reach - Engagement -
Website Visitors - Ad Clicks - Conversion Rate

Include campaign/project name, platform, reporting period, verified
metrics, and optional report/evidence link.

**Critical trust requirement:** Do not invent metrics, clients,
testimonials, growth percentages, or screenshots. Do not present demo
values as actual results. The initial page must show this empty state:

**Your results will appear here.**

*We believe in transparent reporting. Verified campaign results will be
published here when available.*

If demo data is shown in the design, visibly label it **DEMO --- NOT
ACTUAL CLIENT RESULTS**. Reserve a clear structure for adding real
verified campaign results later.

## 8. Page 5 --- About Us (`/about`)

Title: **CREATIVITY MEETS DIGITAL GROWTH**

Sections: - Agency introduction. - Mission and vision. - Creative
philosophy. - Marketing and technology approach. - Our workflow. - Why
choose us. - CTA to get a free consultation.

Use premium editorial layouts and subtle motion. Keep company history,
team members, awards, and experience figures as editable placeholders
until real details are provided. Do not fabricate credentials.

## 9. Page 6 --- Contact / Free Consultation (`/contact`)

Title: **LET'S GROW YOUR BUSINESS**

Supporting copy: *Get a free consultation and discover how digital
marketing can support your business goals.*

### Consultation form

Fields: - Full Name (required) - Business Email (required) - Phone
Number (required) - Business / Brand Name (optional) - Website URL
(optional) - Services Required (multi-select) - Approximate Marketing
Budget (optional) - Project Details (required) - Preferred Contact
Method

Submit CTA: **GET FREE CONSULTATION**

Design required-field validation, accessible error states, loading
state, success state, and failure state. The success state must only be
used after a real submission succeeds; the design itself should not
imply backend integration is already configured.

### WhatsApp CTA

Button label: **Chat on WhatsApp**

Configured number: **+91 9305856475**

Link: `https://wa.me/919305856475`

Optional prefilled message: *Hello, I would like a free digital
marketing consultation for my business.*

Make the CTA prominent and mobile-friendly. The visitor must send the
message themselves.

### Instagram link

Add an **Follow us on Instagram** button with an Instagram icon. Keep
the profile URL as a clearly named configurable placeholder because the
actual profile URL has not been provided. Do not invent an Instagram
username.

Include editable placeholders for business email, phone, and other
social links.

## 10. Page 7 --- Insights / Blog (`/insights`)

Create a premium digital marketing insights page with: - Featured
article. - Article cards. - Search. - Category filters. - Article detail
layout. - Related articles. - Reading time.

Categories: Meta Ads · SEO & Local SEO · Google Business Profile ·
Website Development · Social Media Marketing · AI Video Creation.

Use editable sample educational content. Do not present fictional client
case studies as real.

## 11. Shared footer

Include: - DIGITAL MARKETING logo and short description. - Quick
navigation. - Services links. - Our Creative Work and Our Results. -
Contact details. - WhatsApp CTA. - Instagram and other configured social
links. - Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions. - Dynamic copyright
year.

## 12. Responsive and accessibility requirements

-   Desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
-   Test widths around 320, 375, 390, and 430 pixels.
-   No horizontal overflow.
-   Touch-friendly controls and mobile menu.
-   Accessible labels and visible keyboard focus.
-   Semantic heading hierarchy and adequate contrast.
-   Reduced-motion alternative.
-   Responsive video modal.
-   Lazy-load below-the-fold media and use poster images.

## 13. Deliverables expected from Stitch

Generate a cohesive multi-page UI concept with: - All seven page
designs. - Shared navigation and footer. - Consistent typography,
colors, spacing, buttons, cards, and icon style. - Home hero and
creative-video section. - Six service cards. - Portfolio filters and
video modal design. - Results page with honest empty state. - Contact
form and WhatsApp CTA. - Responsive desktop and mobile variants where
possible.

Prioritize visual consistency and high-fidelity UI. Use reusable
components and clearly named layers/components wherever the tool
supports them.

**Important implementation note:** Treat this as the UI/design
specification. Real video playback, form submission, database storage,
and analytics verification must be connected and tested in the
development environment; a generated visual design alone does not
implement those backend behaviors.
